# bliss-beta-by-12do121-roblox.github.io
bliss beta by 12do121 on roblox

a small roblox game

Bliss [BETA] by 12do121 was created in May 2024, on roblox.

The game was a showcase game that contained many details that was reflected from the original alpha versions[1] design.

The game contains gears that you could collect with the in-game currency "bobuks" each gear gives the player a different effect. Some gears like bloxflakes, jb cola or the glider could be used for movement. Allowing the player to reach new areas of the map. This made the game not just be a showcase game and lean into more movement styled game play.

The movement was simple or sometimes complex. List of movement:

-wall ride: allows the player to glide across walls with speed based on walk speed.

-phantom fase: a big that let you go through walls using jb cola.

-platforms boost (unofficial name, admins only)

-glider boost: jumping with a glider in the climbing fase boosts the character backwards

-wallhop: facing away from a wall completely with jb cola active

-bloxflakes: a "frame" for tricks, boosting the player upward

-combo: jumping while in the climbing fase gives you a score

(you get gears in the bliss sewers area)

The lore of the game is dark, dramatic. The game looks to be a normal town but holds deep secrets in the background.

The glitch: the player 'nikosgamerfuture' has an effect where if he doesn't drink bloxy cola for 2 minutes he becomes the glitch, an entity that runs off anger.

The formation: a company and a group of people who created the glitch by running a roblox player called 'nikosgamerfuture' through a unauthorized experiment.

Npcs: normal npcs that are secretly sentient and are being forced to be quiet by the formation
---------------------
BUILDINGS

the 'index' building allows players to change there bliss avatar, it only has classic roblox hats, t-shirts, and an ability to change your characters gender

the 'hq building' holds a yearly event called 'voting day' where one king would be chosen by the voting on the players, started in 2023. (winners: 2023:12do121 2024:1000velo2 2025:yousefrdm 2026:nikosgamerfuture)

the 'cola factory' a factory where cola is made but not obtained. (you get cola from the sewers, or you can get 2x cola from a secret npc found near the bliss train station and space station)

the 'bloxflakes building' is a place where bloxflakes is obtained, bloxflakes allows you to boost yourself upwards.
